# W3 Hebrew lines for native review

Every Hebrew line W3 added, with its English meaning. Written in the plural or with no "you" that needs a gender, no score, no praise line in the kindness quest. Please mark any line that sounds stiff, wrong for the age (5 to 11) or gendered. Sources: `mods/nagmon/quests/*.yaml`, `rules.yaml`, `app/src/ui/journal/words.ts`, `app/src/ui/pc/words.ts` (board lines), `app/src/i18n.ts` (`choiceLater`).
